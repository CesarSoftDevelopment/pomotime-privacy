Privacy Policy — Pomo Time

Last updated: October 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Pomo Time app ("app") handles information when you use it.

Pomo Time is developed and maintained by Cesar Soft Development ("developer", "we").

  • The app has no account, login or sign-up.
  • Your tasks, timer settings and progress are stored only on your device. We do not have a server that receives them.
  • The app shows ads through Google AdMob, which may process your advertising ID, IP address and device information.
  • We do not sell personal data.

1. About Pomo Time

Pomo Time is a timer based on the Pomodoro technique. With the app you can:

  • Run pomodoro, short break and long break timers;
  • Create, edit and delete tasks and set a pomodoro goal for each one;
  • Adjust durations, automatic start, alarm sound, volume and reminders;
  • Receive notifications from the running timer and when a task is finished;
  • Use the app for free with advertising.

2. Data stored on your device

The information below is created by you or by the app and is saved locally, in the app's private storage on your device. It is not sent to the developer.

DataPurpose
Tasks: title, description, pomodoro goal, completed pomodoros and whether the task is doneShow and manage your task list
Timer settings: durations, automatic start, long break interval, reminder, alarm sound, volume and repetitionsKeep your preferences
Timer progress: number of pomodoros and breaks completedShow your current cycle
App state: whether the introduction was completed, whether permissions were already requested, and how many times the app was openedAvoid repeating screens and decide when an opening ad may be shown (never on the first launch)

Device backup

If backup or device-to-device transfer is enabled in your Android settings, the operating system may include the app's local data in the backup of your own Google account. This backup is managed by Google and by you. The developer has no access to it.

3. Data processed for advertising

The app shows banner ads and an ad when the app is opened, provided by Google AdMob.

To display, measure and protect ads against fraud, Google may collect and process, depending on your device, region and consent choices:

  • Advertising ID;
  • IP address, which may be used to estimate your approximate location;
  • Device information, such as model, manufacturer, operating system and version;
  • App information, such as version and diagnostic data;
  • Interactions with ads, such as views and clicks.

This data is collected directly by Google's software inside the app. The developer does not receive the advertising ID and does not use it to identify you.

More information:

  • Google Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy
  • How Google uses data from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • Google advertising technologies: policies.google.com/technologies/ads

Consent for advertising

Where required by law, such as in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the app uses the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) to show a consent form before ads are requested. In those regions, ads are only requested after the form has been answered.

You can also control ad personalization on your device at any time:

  • In Android settings, under Google > Ads (or Privacy > Ads), you can reset or delete your advertising ID;
  • Clearing the app's data in Android settings removes the stored consent choice, and the form is shown again where applicable.

4. Other Google services

Google Play in-app updates

The app asks Google Play whether a newer version is available and may prompt you to update. This check is handled by Google Play, which already knows the Google account and device used to install the app.

Firebase

The app includes Firebase software libraries from Google. The current version of the app does not use them to create accounts, or to store or send your tasks, settings or any other content. These libraries may process technical information needed for their own operation, subject to Google's privacy policy.

5. Data the app does not request

Pomo Time does not ask for or access:

  • Name, e-mail address or phone number;
  • Precise location (GPS);
  • Contacts, photos, videos or files;
  • Camera or microphone;
  • Payment information.

6. Sharing of data

The developer does not sell personal data and does not have a server that stores user data.

The only sharing that occurs is the processing carried out by Google through the services described in sections 3 and 4 (Google AdMob, Google User Messaging Platform, Google Play and Firebase libraries).

Because Google operates worldwide, this information may be processed on servers located in countries other than yours, according to Google's policies and applicable legal mechanisms.

7. Purposes and legal basis

Data is processed to:

  • Provide the app's features (timer, tasks, settings and notifications);
  • Display and measure ads, which keep the app free;
  • Keep the app up to date and working correctly.

Where applicable, processing is based on the performance of the service requested by you, on legitimate interest, and on your consent where the law requires it, especially for advertising. This includes the Brazilian General Data Protection Law (Law No. 13,709/2018 — LGPD) and the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

8. Retention and deletion

Data stored on your device is kept until you delete it. You can delete it at any time by:

  • Deleting tasks inside the app;
  • Clearing the app's data in Android settings;
  • Uninstalling the app.

Since the developer does not store your data on a server, there is nothing for us to delete on our side. Data processed by Google for advertising is retained according to Google's policies.

9. Security

Local data is kept in the app's private storage, protected by the Android operating system and not accessible to other apps. Communications with Google's services use encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS).

No storage or transmission method is completely secure, so absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

10. Your rights

Depending on the law that applies to you, you may have the right to request confirmation of processing, access, correction, deletion, information about sharing, and withdrawal of consent.

Because the app has no accounts and the developer does not hold your data, most of these rights are exercised directly on your device, as described in sections 3 and 8. For data processed by Google, you can also use the tools Google provides in your Google account.

Requests and questions can be sent to: cesardeveloper0110@gmail.com

11. Children

Pomo Time is not directed to children. The app does not knowingly request personal data from children.
